threads-ai

Content analytics and publishing assistant for Threads creators.

What it does

threads-ai helps an individual creator understand what they publish on Threads and decide what to publish next. It collects the creator's own posts and performance metrics over time, tracks which topics and formats are currently gaining traction, and drafts candidate posts that the creator reviews and approves before anything is published.

Features

  • Performance history — records views, likes, replies, reposts and quotes for each post as a time series, so early reach can be distinguished from final reach.
  • Audience growth tracking — samples follower count on a schedule and attributes growth to individual posts.
  • Topic discovery — searches public Threads content by keyword and topic tag to surface what is currently being discussed, so drafts stay relevant.
  • Draft assistance — generates candidate posts grounded in the creator's own writing. Every draft requires explicit human approval before publishing.
  • Reply management — surfaces replies and mentions worth responding to. Replies are written by the person, not automated.

Permissions used

  • threads_basic — read the user's own profile and posts.
  • threads_content_publish — publish posts the user has approved.
  • threads_manage_insights — read performance metrics for the user's own content.
  • threads_read_replies, threads_manage_replies — read and respond to replies on the user's posts.
  • threads_manage_mentions — surface public posts that mention the user.
  • threads_keyword_search — search public Threads content by keyword and topic tag to identify trending subjects.
  • threads_profile_discovery — look up public profiles and their public posts for topic research.

Data handling

Data is stored locally on the user's own machine. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Content retrieved from the Threads API is used solely to produce analytics and drafts for the authenticated user.
