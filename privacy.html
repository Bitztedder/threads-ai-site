Privacy Policy

threads-ai · Last updated 22 September 2026

1. Who we are

threads-ai is an analytics and publishing assistant for Threads creators, operated by 소프트웨어컴퍼니. Contact: jandoe3690@gmail.com.

2. What we access

When you authorize threads-ai with your Threads account, the app accesses:

  • Your Threads profile (user ID, username, profile picture, biography)
  • Your own Threads posts and their text, media type, timestamps and permalinks
  • Performance metrics for your own posts (views, likes, replies, reposts, quotes, shares)
  • Your follower count and, where available, aggregate follower demographics
  • Replies on your posts and public posts that mention you
  • Public Threads content matching keywords or topic tags you specify, and public profiles you choose to research

3. Why we access it

  • To show you how your own content performs over time
  • To identify topics and formats currently gaining traction, so drafts stay relevant
  • To generate draft posts that you review and approve before publishing
  • To publish posts and replies that you have explicitly approved

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling of third parties, or training general-purpose models. Public content retrieved through search is used only to summarize topic trends and is not republished or redistributed.

4. Where data is stored

Data is stored locally, in a database file on the device operated by the authenticated user. Access tokens are stored on the same device with restricted file permissions and are never transmitted to any party other than Meta's own API endpoints.

5. What we never do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share your data with third parties.
  • We do not post, reply, or delete anything without your explicit approval.

6. Retention and deletion

Data is retained only as long as it is useful to the authenticated user, and may be deleted at any time. To request deletion, see Data Deletion. Revoking the app's access from your Threads account settings immediately ends all further data access.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by emailing jandoe3690@gmail.com. Requests are handled within 30 days.

8. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
