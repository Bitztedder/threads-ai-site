Data Deletion

threads-ai · Last updated 22 September 2026

Revoke access

To immediately stop threads-ai from accessing your Threads account, revoke the app's permission from Threads itself:

  1. Open Threads and go to Settings
  2. Select Website permissions
  3. Find threads-ai and remove its access

Revoking access takes effect immediately and prevents any further data retrieval.

Request deletion of stored data

To have any data previously retrieved about your account deleted, email admin@bitztedder.com with:

  • The subject line "Data deletion request"
  • Your Threads username

We confirm receipt within 7 days and complete deletion within 30 days. Deletion covers your profile information, posts, performance metrics, follower history, and any generated drafts associated with your account. A confirmation is sent once deletion is complete.

Scope

Data collected by threads-ai is stored locally on the authenticated user's own device. It is not replicated to third-party servers, so deletion on that device is complete and irreversible.
